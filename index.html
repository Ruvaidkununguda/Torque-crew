01 // ORIGIN & MISSION

Forged in the Lab.
Proven in the Arena.

Torque Crew is an independent competitive robotics team engineered to push beyond conventional limits. When the countdown starts, our alliance delivers.

ENGINEERING WITH NO COMPROMISE

Founded on the principle of relentless engineering, Torque Crew was created to bridge high-level robotics theory with aggressive, real-world match performance. In HyperDrive, matches are won in milliseconds. We optimize every gram of weight, every line of computer vision code, and every driver reflex.

From rapid-prototyping in our CNC workshop to custom PCB routing and sensor fusion, every mechanism on our machine is designed for speed, torque, and survivability under competition collision.

"We don't build robots to participate. We build machines that redefine how HyperDrive matches are played."

DRIVETRAIN & PROPULSION

INDEPENDENT 4-MODULE SWERVE

Equipped with 4 custom-machined swerve modules utilizing dual brushless motors per wheel (one for drive, one for azimuth steering). Enables true holonomic omnidirectional vectoring on the 12×12 ft competition field.

16.4 FT/S MAX SPRINT SPEED
4× BRUSHLESS HIGH-TORQUE MOTORS
0.18 SEC 360° AZIMUTH ROTATION
MANIPULATOR & SCORING

HIGH-COMPRESSION ROLLER INTAKE

Features custom 45A durometer compliant silicone wheels with an active centrifugal indexer. Ingests COREs from any approach angle and prevents jams with automatic current-spike reversal.

0.32 SEC INTAKE CYCLE TIME
3× CORES MAX CAPACITY BUFFER
4,500 RPM FEED WHEEL SPEED
AUTONOMOUS & SENSORS

DUAL APRILTAG STEREO VISION

Dual monochrome global-shutter cameras track competition field tags with sub-centimeter accuracy. Integrated 6-DOF IMU fuses wheel odometry and optical flow into a continuous state estimator.

90 FPS DETECTION REFRESH
±0.5 CM ODOMETRY ACCURACY
30.0 SEC AUTONOMOUS PRE-ROUTINE
END GAME & LEVEL UP

DUAL TELESCOPING WINCH RIG

Multi-stage carbon-fiber tubes powered by a constant-force spring and high-speed winch. Deploys at T-minus 30 seconds to lock onto the Space Station rung with mechanical anti-backdrive ratchets.

1.8 SEC DEPLOYMENT TIME
180 LBF WINCH LIFT CAPACITY
LEVEL 3 MAX ALLIANCE HANG
