HOW TO ADD / REPLACE YOUR REAL TEAM PHOTOS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A (Standard / Permanent):
  1. Save your photos in `assets/team/` using matching names:
     - `assets/team/ahmed-damda.jpg`
     - `assets/team/ahmed-akrami.jpg`
     - `assets/team/ahmed-zihaf.jpg`
     - `assets/team/dibaan-sb.jpg`
     - `assets/team/shiham.jpg`
     - `assets/team/shammas.jpg`
     - `assets/team/quaid.jpg`
     - `assets/team/mohammed-hussain.jpg`
  2. In `index.html`, change the `.svg` extension to `.jpg` for that member.
  3. Save and refresh the browser!

Option B (Instant Browser Preview Tool):
  1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
  2. Scroll to "02 // THE ROSTER".
  3. Click "📷 TEST / PREVIEW YOUR PHOTOS".
  4. Choose any team member from the dropdown and pick a photo from your computer.
